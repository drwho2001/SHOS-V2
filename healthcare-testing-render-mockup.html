Healthcare · Testing — card render set

Testing is the reference grammar for card anatomy (title row → byline → summary state line → context groups). Each card carries title, date/time + tense, location, reason for testing, sites/organisms, result, treatment (via clinic visit), follow-up, sample tracking, notes, and attachments. Every capturable form fact is represented on the card — nothing is invented for the card that the form cannot capture. Rendered on a 393dp device that shrinks to fit narrow panes.

States

Summary vocab: BOOKED / PENDING / POSITIVE / NEGATIVE / OVERDUE / VOID. Dot colour = state. Treatment only appears on positive results (nothing to treat when pending/negative/void). Treatment is recorded through a clinic visit (mode: Given at clinic / Started at clinic / Prescribed / For at home).

The live testing module owns persistence, loading/empty states, native-dialog replacement, and the two-way clinic-visit link (BACKLOG 13). Those are wiring gaps, not card-design defects.
