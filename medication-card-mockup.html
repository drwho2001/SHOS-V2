Shipped anatomy: the same card grammar as encounters/contacts (§10.2.1). Title row: status dot + 15/600 name + ⋮ menu. The byline is now one line carrying quantity and timing together — dose form, total strength per dose and schedule — so the card answers "1 tablet · 200mg/245mg · Daily · 4:00 PM" in one read. When a dose takes more than one unit the strength is stated as a total ("2 tablets · 300mg total"), and ingredient names are kept when the title is a brand ("Zapain · 2 tablets · codeine 60mg/paracetamol 1000mg total"). The last/next dose line moves up to sit directly under that byline, before any numbers. Stock keeps its big number + progress bar under a STOCK group label, with the restock level stated beside the quantity ("restock at 7 tablets") and marked on the track; refill state is chips (red = action needed, grey = days remaining/supplier, blue = mark as requested, or the requested-awaiting chip with its × dismiss). Adherence is three independent tiles — streak, 7-day, this container — each with its own verdict colour (≥80% green, ≥50% amber, below red), so a green streak can sit beside a red container. The streak flame is filled orange-red when the streak is live, and the 7-day tile carries an up/down arrow against the previous 7 days. Buttons stay the two-button row, with `+1 Log dose`.

Layout intent

The card answers in order: what is it and when do I take it (one byline) → where am I up to (last/next dose) → anything about this dose (timing advice, notes) → how much is left (stock number + bar + chips) → how am I doing (three adherence tiles) → what can I tap (buttons). The lockout flow, dose-correction tap, and timing advisory are unchanged behavioural furniture — only their surrounding spacing follows the card grid (10px above each group title, tight 3–4px gaps inside).

Notes

PrEP and Sertraline show the inventory-tracked path. A requested refill replaces the red action chip with the blue "Requested … — awaiting refill" chip (the × dismiss stays chip-level), because once ordered there is nothing left to action. "No container yet" keeps the three-tile rhythm when no refill has been logged rather than repeating the 7-day figure under a second label. Trend arrows need two complete windows; a medication started this week shows none rather than inventing a baseline. Notes stay the italic annotated line with an accent rule, matching encounters' notes treatment. Light-mode and dark-mode tint tokens follow encounters' mockup pairs (blue #5b9bd5 on #5b9bd515; green #5fd9a4; amber #F59E0B; red #ff7a7e; flame #F97316).
