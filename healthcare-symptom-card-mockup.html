Healthcare · Symptom log — consistency audit & proposed card

Symptom log is the closest of the four to the shared anatomy — it already has a status dot, a title, and one meta line rather than a stack. What it lacks is everything after that line: no chip cloud, no footer, no actions, and a different red-border rule. Below: shipped, proposed, and the audit.

Key changes

1) The one-line meta stays as the model for the other three: it becomes the 10px icon byline. 2) Severity moves out of free text into a chip so it is scannable — red/amber/green chips reuse the dot's own colours. 3) Duration or resolution date is added as the footer; today a resolved entry shows no end date at all, so the card cannot answer "how long did that last?". 4) Symptom names become chips, which is what makes a multi-symptom entry readable. 5) Two actions per card. 6) "Older entry" becomes a pill. 7) The fade exemption for active symptoms is preserved and stated in an advisory so nobody "fixes" it later.
