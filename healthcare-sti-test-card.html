Design notes
� Structure: tag to byline to summary to sites/organisms to treatment to schedule.
� Tag colour = indication: routine green, urgent symptoms/positive red, non-urgent (vaccines/contraception) orange.
� Copy is en-GB; non-per-screen strings come from the app's shared string table.
� Summary: lead word caps (NEGATIVE/POSITIVE/PENDING/OVERDUE/BOOKED/VOID). Rest sentence case; HIV always capped.
� Sites and organisms are not 1:1; all tested sites listed; detected chips outlined red.
� Pending card: trigger "Add results" two-phase editor to confirm, then Edit/Revert via three-dots.
� Site picker validates non-blocking: HIV - blood/mouth swab; syphilis - blood; chlamydia/gonorrhoea - urine/swab. Warning, not block.
� Clinically grounded: doxycycline sole first line (BASHH 2026); retest 2-6 months; window behind info dot.
� Female-health visibility: vaginal swab hidden by default in the booked card picker; patient can opt in via toggle.
� Treatment bank: chlamydia (doxy 100mg BD�7d, alt azithro 1g single), gonorrhoea (ceftriaxone 1g IM, alt cefixime 400mg PO), syphilis (benzathine pen 2.4MU IM, alt doxy 100mg BD�14d), HIV PEP (TDF/FTC + raltegravir or dolutegravir �28d), MGen (doxy then moxi), trichomoniasis (metronidazole 400mg BD�7d), BV (metronidazole 400mg BD�5-7d), herpes (aciclovir 400mg TDS�5d).
