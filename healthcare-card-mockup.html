Shipped anatomy: the same card grammar as encounters/contacts (§10.2.1), adapted for Healthcare. Title row: status dot + 15/600 name. The byline is one line carrying quantity and timing together — diagnosis, result, or appointment date plus time. Last/next visit moves up into the same byline as a second chunk (Last result · Next due). Status chips use the healthcare accent (`#09582E`); result chips are green when normal, red when overdue; warning-needed chips use the amber-active tint. Buttons stay two-button, healthcare-accented: filled for primary action (Log test / vaccination / symptom), outline for secondary. The advisory block and note follow the medication card pattern: warning styling, accent border-left. Light-mode tint `#09582E15`, no tint in dark mode — outline only.

Layout intent

The card answers in order: what is it and when do I get it (one byline) → where am I up to (last/next dose/visit) → anything about this entry (timing advice, notes) → how I'm doing (status row + diagnostic chips) → what can I tap (buttons). Advisory blocks and notes sit between the byline and the numbers.

Notes

Cards cover three realistic healthcare states: completed & clean (green, on track), overdue (red alert, catch-up needed), and active/ongoing (healthcare-accented, treatment active). Diagnostic/therapeutic info groups under a DIAGNOSIS / TREATMENT chip group rather than pairing status with generic chips. Urgency state ("overdue since…") sits in the status row alongside the colour marker, not as one more chip among rate/supplier/requested chips. Each sub-screen should render one such card per record.
