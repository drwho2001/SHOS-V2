Vaginal health conditions (BV, candidiasis, trichomoniasis, PID, pregnancy) — separate from the STI list. Urgency follows clinical treatability: PID/cervicitis = same-day urgent; trichomoniasis = prompt; BV/candida = treat as indicated; pregnancy = refer antenatal. Urgency tiers need clinician sign-off before app build.

Cards use the STI grammar (dot, byline, summary, ctx-groups, advisory). Dot colour = urgency: red = urgent, amber = pending/prompt, green = complete/resolved. No partner notification for BV/candida; PID may trigger notification.
