The Contacts module bundles the reviewed Android/Google Play or PWA launcher
images in `public/contact-platform-icons/` so cards work offline and in the APK.
The source-resolution copies remain in `mockup-assets/` for design review.

These are third-party trademarks/artwork shown only to identify user-entered
contact methods; SHOS is not affiliated with or endorsed by those services.
The Twitter/X badge is a small split mark drawn from the app's existing
Phosphor Twitter and X glyphs. Telegram and Messenger have a light-mode shadow
to keep their white-edged art legible; FabGuys/FabSwingers retain their white
tiles, with a subtle neutral edge only in light mode.
