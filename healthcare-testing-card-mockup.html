Key changes

1) One byline replaces the three stacked lines (date, setting, result). 2) Panel and Result become labelled chip groups; the blur of repeated test names becomes scannable chips. 3) A footer states result + next due in the same read position every card uses. 4) Two actions on the card (Log test, Schedule retest / Log clinic visit) instead of none. 5) Older-record fade and the "Older test" pill are kept — the only sub-module that already did this correctly. 6) Red border keeps meaning "positive result" — but that meaning is now declared once in the design manual, not re-invented per sub-module.
