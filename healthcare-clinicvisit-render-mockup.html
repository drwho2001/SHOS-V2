Healthcare · Clinic visits — card render set

Clinic visits is the canonical owner of treatment given/prescribed and follow-up, and a visit can be started from a test or from quick add → New clinic visit. Every capturable form fact is represented on the card (direct, grouped, or as a count/summary) — nothing is invented for the card that the form cannot capture. Rendered in the STI/testing grammar on a 393dp device that shrinks to fit narrow panes.

States

Dot = action state: clinic accent (hc) = booked/upcoming, amber = follow-up due, muted = completed. Summary is a store-once lead-word state line (BOOKED / COMPLETED / DUE); chips never repeat the summary.
