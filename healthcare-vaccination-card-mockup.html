Key changes

1) Date and due date merge into one byline plus a footer — the same two positions the medication card uses for last dose / next dose. 2) A dose-progress bar makes "1 of 2" visible without opening the record; it is currently hidden in the detail sheet only. 3) Chip groups carry status and series. 4) Two card-level actions. 5) The recency treatment is added — Vaccinations was the only list with no fade and no pill, so a 2019 dose competed visually with a dose that is due now. 6) Red border keeps meaning "overdue", now declared once in the design manual instead of being re-decided per sub-module.

Cross-module note

After this change all four healthcare lists read identically at a glance: dot in the title row, one icon byline, an optional progress strip, one footer, grouped chips, two actions. The only per-module differences left are the words in the chips and which colour the dot takes when something needs action.
