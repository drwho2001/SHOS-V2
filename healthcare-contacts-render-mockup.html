Healthcare · Contacts — card render set

Contacts is a detail screen — deliberately outside the list-card grammar (no summary/byline state line). Each card carries name, pronouns, HIV status (stated, not derived), contact methods (phone, email, platforms), availability schedule, location, kinks/limits/chems, relationship type, notes, and linked encounters/testing. Every capturable form fact is represented on the card — nothing is invented for the card that the form cannot capture. Rendered on a 393dp device that shrinks to fit narrow panes.
