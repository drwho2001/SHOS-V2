Key changes

1) A status dot is added — the one sub-module that had none. 2) Date, clinician and location collapse into one byline with icons, replacing up to four stacked 12px lines. 3) "Older visit" becomes a pill in the title row, matching Testing's "Older test" and Symptom Log's "Older entry" — a line of bold text is not the same component. 4) A follow-up/next-appointment footer gives the card the same read position as the medication card. 5) Grouped chips carry visit type and outcome. 6) Two card-level actions; the future appointment also gets Reschedule. 7) The future-appointment exemption from fading is kept and made explicit in an advisory, because it is a real correctness rule, not a style choice.
