Healthcare · Symptom log — card render set

Symptom log carries multiple symptoms per entry, severity, start/resolved dates, linked encounter and test, and notes. Every capturable form fact is represented on the card (direct, grouped, or as a count/summary) — nothing is invented for the card that the form cannot capture. Rendered in the STI/testing grammar on a 393dp device that shrinks to fit narrow panes.

States

Dot = severity while active, green once resolved — unchanged meaning. Severe + active keeps the red border. Active symptoms are never faded by the older-record rule. Summary is a store-once lead-word state line (ACTIVE / RESOLVED); chips never repeat the summary.
